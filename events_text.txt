ROBO RACE 

Eligibility: 

Open to all students with a self-built robot that meets the event specifications. 

Bot Specifications: 

Weight Limit: Maximum 3 KG (including battery).

Dimensions of the bot: Must be less than 280 mm x 280 mm (length x width), with a height of less than 150 mm. 

Power Source: Maximum 16 – 17 V battery. 

Control: Only wireless bots are allowed. Wired bots will not be permitted. 

Safety: The bot must be safe to operate and must not include hazardous components or emit harmful signals. 

Error: Of only 10% will be considered. 

Race Format: 

Bots will compete on a specially designed obstacle track. 

The objective is to complete the track in the given order in the shortest time while maintaining control and precision. 

Time penalties may be applied; 10 sec for every wall touch/collision/off-track, missed checkpoints cost a 30 sec penalty, 2 free hand touch for the certain area of the track and for every hand touch after that will cost 30 sec, 2 skip allowed for any obstacle that costs 45 sec penalties each. 

Judging Criteria: 

Speed: Time taken to complete the track after totaling penalty time. 

Control: Stability and maneuverability of the bot. 

Completion: Successful navigation of all track segments. Judges' decisions are final and binding. 

 

Track Details of ROBO RACE  

 

Description: Robo Race track will be a customized course with various features like speed breakers, ramps, and different terrain surfaces. While specific layouts will be revealed on the day of the competition, a defined starting and finishing line, and a total length of 130-150 feet. Key elements which will include obstacles such as tunnel, curves, a seesaw, a marble pit, and a slippery path designed to challenge robots' ability to navigate different terrains. 

Arena dimensions: A Large size around 45 x 55 square feet, with bot lanes that might start at 35cm wide and be up to 45-55 cm wide at certain checkpoints.

ROBO SOCCER 

Event Description: A manually controlled robot must push the ball into the opponent's goal while defending its own.  

Robot Specifications: 

Size Limit: 300mm x 300mm x 300mm 

Weight: <= 5kg 

Control: Wired/wireless 

Power: Max 16 – 17 V battery (No IC engines) 

Kicking mechanisms prohibited 

Clamps must be fully stationary — no motion, no actuation. 

Error: Of only 10% will be considered. 

Rules and Regulations: 

Match Format: 2 halves of 2 minutes each. 

Timeliness: Late arrivals may face elimination but can rejoin with a penalty. 

Teams switch sides after halftime. 

Tie-breaker: (Golden Goal) if scores are tied. 

Controlled ramming allowed when opponent has the ball. 

Corner trapping for over 5 seconds results in a reset.

Total of 1 minute repair time allowed in case of bot malfunctions.

Arena Details: 

Size: 8.0ft × 5.0ft (Fully covered with artificial grass) with 35cm goalposts.

ROBO SUMO



Eligibility: 

Open to all students with a self-built robot that meets the event specifications. 



Robot Specifications: 

Size Limit: 300mm x 300mm x 300mm 

Weight: <= 5kg 

Control: Wired/wireless 

Power: Max 16 - 17V battery (No IC engines) 

Kicking mechanisms prohibited 

Clamps must be fully stationary — no motion, no actuation. 

Error: Of only 10% will be considered. 

Rules and Regulations: 

Match Format: 3 minutes each. 

Timeliness: Late arrivals may face elimination but can rejoin with a penalty. 

Teams switch sides after halftime. 

Tie-breaker: (Golden Fight) if scores are tied. 

1v1 knockout-style matches held in a square arena (8 ft x 8 ft). 

Objective: Push the opponent’s bot out of the ring.

Total of 1 minute repair time allowed in case of bot malfunctions.

In the absence of a clear winner, judges will determine the outcome based on performance metrics.

Judging Criteria:

Bot Control & Maneuverability

Aggression & Tactical Strategy

Structural Stability & Responsiveness

Judges' decisions are final.

LINE FOLLOWER ROBOT 

Eligibility: 

Open to all students with a self-built robot that meets the event specifications. 

No pre-built carrier boards or kits will be allowed

Bot Specifications: 

Weight Limit: Maximum 1.5 KG (including battery).

Dimensions of the bot: Must be less than 250 mm x 220 mm (length x width), with a height of less than 100 mm. 

Power Source: Maximum 11 – 12 V battery. 

Control: Fully autonomous wireless bots are allowed. 

Sensors: IR or optical sensors only

Indicator: Must include a visible power ON/OFF switch.

Error: Of only 10% will be considered. 

Track Specification: 

Track will consist of a black line on a white background (or vice versa) where Line width will be around 20 mm - 30 mm at almost every point.

Turns: Includes sharp turns, smooth curves, and zig-zag paths. 

Junctions: May include T-junctions, cross junctions, and dead ends.

Surface: Smooth matte finish (no reflections). 

Start/Finish: Clearly marked start and end zones. 

Competition Format:

Each team will get 2 attempts. 

Best timing among attempts will be considered. 

Robot must start only after signal. 

Timer starts when robot crosses the start line. 

Timer stops when robot reaches the finish line.

Penalties:

Off-track: +10 seconds penalty.

Manual touch: +15 seconds penalty per touch. 

Skipping section: +30 seconds penalty. 

Restart: Allowed with +20 seconds penalty. 

Disqualification: 

Found using unfair of remote/manual control 

Unsafe design

Judging Criteria: 

Speed: Time taken to complete the track after totaling penalty time. 

Accuracy: Stability and maneuverability of the bot. 

Completion: Successful navigation of all track segments. Judges' decisions are final and binding. 

Arena Details:

Track size approx.: 8 ft × 12 ft (or larger). 

Indoor environment with controlled lighting. 

No external interference allowed.
